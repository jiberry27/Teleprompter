While listening: swipe up on the script to speed up; swipe down to slow down. Your pace is saved. Stop listening to scroll freely.

Smooth scrolling follows your reading position and holds during pauses or ad-libs. Faster pace lets the text catch up; it will not run far ahead of your voice. Tap a word to choose a new starting point.

Start listening allows microphone access. Stop listening pauses the prompter and releases the microphone. Keep this page visible while reading. Use another device to record your video.

This matches English words and short phrases, not meaning. Small wording changes can catch up at the next matching phrase. If you skip a large section, tap the word where you want to resume. It may take a few words to start moving, and recognition can arrive after a brief delay.

Speech recognition depends on your browser and may require internet access. Your browser may send audio to its speech provider. This page adds no paid API, account, subscription, or audio recording.
